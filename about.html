About Me

Interior & Graphic Designer

Hi, I’m Byron, an interior and graphic designer with a passion for creating modern, functional, and visually engaging spaces and designs. I enjoy combining creativity and practical thinking to develop designs that are both attractive and meaningful.

My Background

My background in interior design has helped me develop a strong understanding of space planning, materials, colors, lighting, furniture, drafting, and visual communication. Through my studies and work experience, I have gained experience developing design concepts, creating drawings, preparing presentations, and communicating ideas through visual design.

I am interested in both interior and graphic design because they allow me to explore creativity in different ways. I enjoy working with layouts, typography, colors, shapes, images, and materials to create a clear and balanced visual experience.

My Interests

I am interested in modern, minimalist, Nordic, and mid-century design. I enjoy studying architecture, furniture, materials, lighting, and different approaches to creating comfortable and functional spaces. I am also interested in branding, photography, typography, and visual storytelling.

My Hobbies

Outside of design, I enjoy exploring new places, looking at architecture and interiors, photography, cycling, and discovering new ideas and creative inspiration. I also enjoy visiting furniture stores, galleries, cafés, and interesting spaces to see how design is used in everyday life.

My Design Goal

My goal is to create designs that are not only visually appealing but also practical, comfortable, and connected to the people who use them. I am always looking for opportunities to learn new skills, experiment with new ideas, and continue developing my own design style.
